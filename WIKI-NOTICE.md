# RuneScape Wiki-derived data outside open-f2p

The facts in the files below are adapted from the RuneScape Wiki,
https://runescape.wiki/, by its contributors. They are distributed under
Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported
(CC BY-NC-SA 3.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/

Where the build recorded them, records cite their source page title or URL.
Changes: select the rows this picker uses; join Wiki facts to game-cache ids,
chunk coordinates and task rows; normalise names, membership flags, rates and
weights; add project-written review notes and corrections. Some files mix
these facts with Jagex game-cache data or original-picker data; see
THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md for those parts.

# Source and method membership review — October 8, 2026

This review maps exact retained source labels to pinned RuneScape Wiki revisions. It supplements geographic access and item membership; it does not infer a source's membership from its chunk, move sources, replace quantities, create routes, or change player facts.

## Coverage and evidence

The intrinsic catalogue covers 11,402 exact runtime labels from 7,893 distinct requested Wiki titles. The bounded gatherer completed 198 batches of at most 40 titles, sequentially, without retries. Four additional targeted API batches cover drop tables, recipe variants, the onyx supply distinction, Demon Boss aliases, incidental loot, Slayer assignment access, and wicked hood acquisition. One preliminary six-title API smoke lookup was also made; those subjects are captured again in the main/targeted snapshots. All runtime evidence snapshots and their byte hashes live under `.local/game-data/source-membership-2026-10-08-v1`. Each dataset records the retained reference hash, generator hashes, and snapshot manifest/input hashes. Repository text hashes normalize CRLF to Git LF; external snapshot hashes preserve original bytes.

Task coverage here means quest steps/completions and labelled spell/prayer methods. It is not an audit of every processing recipe or skill method. The separate row catalogue has 536 restrictions: 155 shop-stock rows, 364 monster/table drop rows, four Shaft recipe variants, ten advanced Slayer assignment methods, two explicitly reviewed Demon Boss labels, and Sir Renitee's unique supplier shop alias. These row restrictions supplement the intrinsic catalogue rather than being included in its counts.

The parser requires a matching typed infobox, an exact source-name identity, and unanimous recognized Yes/No values from its top-level `members` fields. NPCs require an NPC infobox; a Monster page cannot certify an NPC source. Generic scenery is excluded from global enforcement unless the exact Wiki object IDs match the independently reviewed typed tree/ore definitions. Source pages with an explicit disambiguation remain unresolved unless exact reviewed cache definition IDs independently corroborate their identity. These guards deliberately exclude Chaos altar, Closed chest, Rock face, Opening, Statue, and NPC Wizard from global source bans. The dataset retains the original member fields and canonical revision as review evidence.

The parser excludes nested recipe fields, normalizes template type case, and leaves mixed variants, unsupported page types, missing pages, and unfamiliar values unresolved. Unknown evidence does not become a guessed member restriction. A free infobox cannot override an existing location-specific restriction, such as the member part of Edgeville Dungeon. The 43 approved member Object labels carry their exact Wiki/cache IDs and resource classes; generic facilities, obstacles, chests, and statues need location-specific identity review.

A blanket member-area field is also withheld when a pinned free Quest infobox has an exact authored `Chunks` location in that named region. This contradictory evidence indicates a free quest path or mixed rooms, not complete member exclusivity. It does not create access or remove source/room gates. Land of Snow retains its original member-infobox evidence alongside the free Violet quests; Sabbot's Cave retains its member-infobox evidence alongside free Death Plateau. Both require a precise room policy before a global member-area ban could be justified.

## Material distinctions

- [Magic tree, revision 37158111](https://runescape.wiki/w/Special:PermanentLink/37158111), declares the tree members-only even though its Shrine of Inanna location rows say `mem=no`. Tree interaction must be checked separately from geographic admission. Magic logs' free item flag cannot certify a free ironman source.
- [King Black Dragon, revision 37188001](https://runescape.wiki/w/Special:PermanentLink/37188001), is free, but its Magic logs drop appears only in the Members table. [Rare drop table, revision 37126411](https://runescape.wiki/w/Special:PermanentLink/37126411), explicitly marks Magic logs members-only. [Gem drop table, revision 36827234](https://runescape.wiki/w/Special:PermanentLink/36827234), explicitly excludes its Rare-table branch for F2P. The retained flattened Gem table therefore needs downstream row restrictions as well as ordinary item flags. Direct free gem rows remain available.
- [Shaft, revision 37171926](https://runescape.wiki/w/Special:PermanentLink/37171926), marks the mahogany, yew, magic, and elder log recipe variants members-only. The resulting Shaft item is free; output membership alone misses this method restriction.
- [Gunnar's Ground, revision 37177980](https://runescape.wiki/w/Special:PermanentLink/37177980), explicitly permits free players to engrave jewellery obtainable by members. The retained onyx engraving task omits its required item. It receives F2P-only guarded required-item metadata, not an intrinsic membership flag; the item membership policy checks [Onyx amulet, revision 37027868](https://runescape.wiki/w/Special:PermanentLink/37027868).
- [Rune Essence mine, revision 37180851](https://runescape.wiki/w/Special:PermanentLink/37180851), is free and lists Carwen Essencebinder, Aubury, and Archmage Sedridor as free entrances. Its rock page has mixed rune/pure variants, so a global rock restriction would be wrong. The retained pure-essence mining task already has `Not F2P`; the ordinary rune-essence method must survive.
- [Acadia logs, revision 37161702](https://runescape.wiki/w/Special:PermanentLink/37161702), are free, have the Light option, and supply ordinary Firemaking XP. Bee/amber outputs are expressly occasional loot. A guarded F2P policy prevents their member-only incidental table from banning the underlying fire-making method, while product admission still removes member loot. [Oaken key, revision 37178543](https://runescape.wiki/w/Special:PermanentLink/37178543), is similarly a bonus find while chopping any trees around [free Wendlewick, revision 37194324](https://runescape.wiki/w/Special:PermanentLink/37194324); its membership must not ban ordinary cutting. Every input, location, source and intrinsic method gate still applies.
- [Slayer, revision 37197819](https://runescape.wiki/w/Special:PermanentLink/37197819), explicitly limits nonmember assignments to Turael and Jacquelyn and training to level 5. All ten declared advanced assignment methods receive independent member-method restrictions; their NPCs' presence or the currency's status cannot certify free assignments. [Slayer points, revision 37162661](https://runescape.wiki/w/Special:PermanentLink/37162661), is a free Currency entity, specifically obtainable through Jacquelyn, despite a same-name member physical cache token. The item-data implementation owns this typed-entity distinction.
- [Tam McGrubor, revision 37124239](https://runescape.wiki/w/Special:PermanentLink/37124239), is a free NPC but explicitly distributes the [member wicked hood, revision 37124398](https://runescape.wiki/w/Special:PermanentLink/37124398), to members; the stock row also says members Yes. No acquisition exception is warranted.
- [Sir Renitee, revision 37179191](https://runescape.wiki/w/Special:PermanentLink/37179191), is a member NPC with an explicitly documented painting/map supplier and heraldic crest service. The retained `Sir Renitee's Construction Supplies` shop label has no separate Wiki Shop page; its reviewed supplier alias therefore has a member source policy backed by the NPC page and retained stock. This restriction applies to that supplier, not the item's existence or acquisition elsewhere.
- Green dragons, blue dragons, Chaos Elemental, and King Black Dragon have free intrinsic infobox flags in the captured revisions. Deadly red spiders remain globally free while the existing Edgeville-specific exception still applies. Demon Boss has two explicitly supported member aliases derived from its infobox name and Wilderness variant, resolving an otherwise missing/case-sensitive page lookup.

## Copied-cache boundaries

The decoded NPC artifact contains 32,905 records, all with `members:null`, and explicitly reports zero verified membership flags, spawn locations, and drop tables. Its 110 records with member actions do not establish whole-NPC membership. Combat/name metadata cannot close these source gaps.

The decoded object catalogue has 141,240 definitions, preserving `is_members`/`isMembers` candidates and member actions with a classification that warns geographic/server evidence is still needed. The places artifact contains map labels, coordinate mappings and lodestone candidates, not an NPC/object or entrance authorization policy. The recipe artifact preserves item production definitions, member-marked product/tool/input candidates and grouped alternatives; it does not establish all server processing rules. Existing reviewed quest/achievement metadata remains in force. Per-step Wiki quest evidence adds a separate membership check without replacing authored quest locations, dependencies, rewards, or IDs.

## Runtime integration contract

`sourceMembershipBlocker(kind, source, f2p, category='')` supplies intrinsic source/area/task policies and local row policies. For row checks, use `category=shop`, `category=monster`, or each nested table name in drop lineage. Intrinsic Task checks use the exact retained category and label. Source collection and named-area admission check this alongside existing source-specific policies; the engine checks Task policy before admitting a method or training capability.

`sourceMembershipRequiredItems(category,label,record,f2p)` returns reviewed omitted supply names only when the exact old-value guard matches. Feed these into the F2P item membership check. Every helper returns no restriction when F2P is off. No all-mode task metadata mutation is required.

`sourceMembershipIncidentalOutput(category,label,record,f2p)` identifies two reviewed methods with exact retained field guards. It waives only the all-member item-output task ban, while mandatory item blockers and normal filtering of every produced item remain in force. It returns false in F2P-off mode.

The focused tests cover geographic/source separation, mixed rock evidence, free monsters, explicit drop/stock restrictions, nested table lineage, intrinsic recipe/spell/prayer methods, the free engraving distinction, guard drift, template parser case/nesting, and F2P-off behavior. The complete integrated regression suite is owned by the parent implementation.

The existing pinned Garden Centre page explicitly says Topiary hedge (bagged) was removed on July 13, 2026. Existing item-alias review snapshots likewise identify Racing boots and Starting horn as removed in 2014, and Super large egg as removed in February 2026. These are stale content questions, not evidence of member-only current stock. They do not receive membership bans in this review. Timepiece is a state-family alias with ordinary free-shop stock; Gold amulet(unstrung) lacks a matching canonical item ID in the residual reviewed alias set. Ceremonial sword names a members-only activity, while the Artisans' Workshop Reward Shop explicitly permits free purchases but limits use of several reward upgrades. Its ambiguous retained stock label cannot be safely converted into a member-only purchase flag.

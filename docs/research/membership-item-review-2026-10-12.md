## Pipeline and limits

`node scripts/research/membership-item-review.mjs gather` makes five sequential bounded Wiki API requests (20, 7, 10, 8 and 1 titles) into ignored `.local/game-data/membership-review-2026-10-12-v1`. `node scripts/research/membership-item-review.mjs` builds the [versioned overlay](../../app/data/item-membership-review.2026-10-12.v1.json) offline, requiring typed Item IDs/flags or exact reviewed evidence statements. It records snapshot byte hashes, pinned revision timestamps/content hashes, LF-normalized generator/reference/catalogue hashes, and the 60 exact retained method records. Existing generated catalogues and the original export remain immutable.

Default lookups consult this overlay; custom catalogues remain isolated. Physical flags and virtual entities are separate. Source admission still applies independently. Nothing and Ceremonial sword are explicit `not-applicable` item classifications, not unknown booleans guessed to be free.

This review records historical membership but deliberately does not remove every obsolete item/source/quest row. That broader stale-content cleanup needs its own task dependency review. It also does not invent free access to a reward shop, replace quantities with modern stock, change saved progress, or assert a live game-server check.

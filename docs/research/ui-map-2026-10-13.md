Frequency labels are estimates for Player 1 and Player 2’s ordinary play, not usage measurements. A “control family” is one authored input, button, tab, menu trigger or disclosure trigger. Repeated tasks, chunks, sources, pins and equipment rows use labelled templates. A family can produce many controls, and different families can be mutually exclusive.

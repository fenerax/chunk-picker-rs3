## Q13. The Boss rule

**One-sentence original meaning:** `Boss` permits task item sources from exact names in the retained boss dictionary; disabling it filters those drops rather than prohibiting all boss encounters, training or obligations, while unchecking it in the original UI also clears `Hard Mode Bosses` (`reference/original-picker/worker.js:776`, `reference/original-picker/index.js:508`, `reference/original-picker/index.js:970`, `reference/original-picker/index.js:12572–12587`).

### Classification holes and task-output exceptions

The retained `bossMonsters` dictionary has **50 exact keys** (export `rs3-chunkpicker-chunkinfo-export.json:239371–239422`). It includes Araxxor, grouped Dagannoth Kings and Legiones, but omits Araxxi, individual kings/Legiones, hard variants and Zemouregal & Vorkath. Neither engine expands the classification through the broader Reaper mappings (`reference/original-picker/worker.js:776`, `app/tasks/access.mjs:403`, `app/tasks/collection.mjs:247`, `app/tasks/collection.mjs:272`; mapping examples export `:239208–239214`, `:239292–239298`). Thus “boss” means dictionary membership here, not every in-game boss.

`bossLogs` is **empty** (export `rs3-chunkpicker-chunkinfo-export.json:239370`). Its original task-output gate (`reference/original-picker/worker.js:3149`) therefore rejects no outputs with this dataset. The recreation has no corresponding gate (`app/tasks/engine.mjs:305–339`). This currently preserves an exception rather than creating a new global Boss ban; a future populated bossLogs dictionary would diverge.

For example, the non-primary Magister Slayer task requires Slayer115, a Key to the Crossing and the monster, then outputs The Magister (export `rs3-chunkpicker-chunkinfo-export.json:64203–64211`). Its loot is in `skillItems.Slayer` (export `:335464`), with no ordinary direct drops record. Subject to its task conditions, those item outputs survive Boss=false in both pipelines (`reference/original-picker/worker.js:3149`, `app/tasks/engine.mjs:305–339`), while generated Every Drop/All Droptables goals are separately blocked (`reference/original-picker/worker.js:5146`, `reference/original-picker/worker.js:5171`, `app/tasks/collection.mjs:170`, `app/tasks/collection.mjs:272`). Manual item/equipment declarations also bypass the Boss/drop predicate in both (`reference/original-picker/worker.js:10301–10305`, `reference/original-picker/worker.js:10858–10864`, `app/tasks/engine.mjs:166–168`).

Boss Collection Log has **502 declared rows**, **73 also tagged Pets**. **497** use only Category, Label and Items: a boss name in the displayed log label generally does not enforce that boss as the acquisition source. Five exceptions add conditions: dragon 2h sword alternate-source/nonshop restrictions for Chaos Elemental, Giant mole and Kalphite Queen (export `rs3-chunkpicker-chunkinfo-export.json:142894`, `:143452`, `:143629`), Bisdi's retained Shadow Reef Story Mode chunk requirement (`:143306`), and Mazcab codex's Beastmaster requirement (`:144046`). Generic item/source checks, rather than an inferred log-owner kill condition, govern these (`reference/original-picker/worker.js:3841–3843`, `reference/original-picker/worker.js:4132–4136`, `app/tasks/engine.mjs:196–218`, `app/tasks/engine.mjs:248`).

## Q4. Non-numeric drop rates

**The original does not translate Rare/Common/Very rare into estimated probabilities.** Its branches use JavaScript coercion, NaN bypasses, slash-prefix parsing and several special heuristics. A drop can pass source admission yet be secondary; different branches can reach different answers (`reference/original-picker/worker.js:775`, `reference/original-picker/worker.js:780`, `reference/original-picker/worker.js:813`, `reference/original-picker/worker.js:818`, `reference/original-picker/worker.js:3149`, `reference/original-picker/worker.js:3222`, `reference/original-picker/worker.js:3244–3251`).

### Ordinary rates and flags

With `rate` standing for the retained source/item/quantity leaf string, the ordinary rarity expression is:

```js
parseFloat(rate.split('/')[0].replaceAll('~', ''))
  / parseFloat(rate.split('/')[1])
```

A direct monster row passes when `rules['Rare Drop']` is truthy **or** this result is NaN **or** it is strictly `> rare cutoff` (`reference/original-picker/worker.js:813–814`; repetitions `reference/original-picker/worker.js:2540`, `reference/original-picker/worker.js:10377`, `reference/original-picker/worker.js:10594`, `reference/original-picker/worker.js:10684`). Passing this predicate does not bypass access, Boss, source unlocks or backlog.

The **actual original UI exposes an amount, not a separate checkbox**, for Rare Drop and Secondary Primary (`reference/original-picker/index.js:11789–11791`, `reference/original-picker/index.js:11856–11858`, `reference/original-picker/index.js:11875–11877`). Its generic handler reads a nonexistent checkbox and assigns their flags undefined (`reference/original-picker/index.js:12570`), including when opening Rules (`reference/original-picker/index.js:11901`) and after applying presets (`reference/original-picker/index.js:11704–11708`). Calculation after a rule edit receives falsey flags (`reference/original-picker/index.js:12591`); saving normalizes undefined rule values to false (`reference/original-picker/index.js:13663–13666`). A supplied/saved true flag can bypass the worker cutoff, but this is not a normal independent original checkbox choice. The recreation exposes boolean and amount separately (`app/web/settings-panel.mjs:67–77`) and defaults absent Rare Drop to enabled in engine checks (`app/tasks/engine.mjs:13`, `app/tasks/engine.mjs:313`).

Direct monster primary classification admits one of:

1. exact `Always`;
2. denominator `parseInt(...) >50` and `isNaN(rate.replaceAll('/', '').replaceAll('@', ''))`;
3. a fractionless rate when cutoff `<1`, or parsed rate `>= cutoff`.

See `reference/original-picker/worker.js:818–821` and repetitions `reference/original-picker/worker.js:2545`, `reference/original-picker/worker.js:10382`, `reference/original-picker/worker.js:10403`, `reference/original-picker/worker.js:10472`, `reference/original-picker/worker.js:10599`, `reference/original-picker/worker.js:10620`, `reference/original-picker/worker.js:10689`, `reference/original-picker/worker.js:10837`. Consequently ordinary labels, blank and bare 256 can become primary already at **1/2**, even before the >50 heuristic applies. Exact Always is special; lowercase always is ordinary text. No rarity-label ordering distinguishes Never from Common.

### Nested tables and specialized branches

Nested original rarity checks **parent NaN**, not combined NaN: `flag || isNaN(parentProbability) || parentProbability * childProbability > cutoff` (`reference/original-picker/worker.js:775`; repetitions `reference/original-picker/worker.js:2502`, `reference/original-picker/worker.js:3155`, `reference/original-picker/worker.js:10339`, `reference/original-picker/worker.js:10556`). Thus **Always→numeric child bypasses rarity**, because Always is NaN in that fraction parser; **numeric parent→opaque child fails** the product comparison unless another route admits it. Nested primary uses exact Always checks, >50 text heuristics or numeric composed `>=` (`reference/original-picker/worker.js:780–783`, `reference/original-picker/worker.js:3167`, `reference/original-picker/worker.js:3189`, `reference/original-picker/worker.js:10344`, `reference/original-picker/worker.js:10561`). Some composition expressions multiply raw strings before parseFloat, so annotations that work in direct parsing can fail here (`reference/original-picker/worker.js:780`, `reference/original-picker/worker.js:788`, `reference/original-picker/worker.js:809–810`).

## Q12. Pending two-choice rolls

**Original policy: preserve offered potential IDs through ordinary edits; resolve by choice, discard on URL import, restore on loading. There is no general eligibility revalidation or automatic replacement.** The recreation similarly freezes offers, adds cancel/undo and rejects unsafe operations, but currently permits accepting a chunk that the partner has already unlocked (`reference/original-picker/index.js:2986–3031`, `reference/original-picker/index.js:4714–4725`, `reference/original-picker/index.js:13215–13232`, `app/core/state.mjs:894–927`).

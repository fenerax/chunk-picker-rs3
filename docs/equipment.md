# Equipment goals

`evaluateEquipment(result, state, memberId, definitions)` combines ordinary armour/accessory/prayer comparisons with coupled weapon loadouts. It consumes only the equipment the task engine confirmed obtainable and equipable through reachable training. It does not create ownership or current-level facts. Crafted-item restrictions, equipment requirements and source gates remain the engine's responsibility.

**Non-permanent BiS** defaults on, preserving existing equipment choices. Disable it in Chunk Rules beside the other Best in Slot rules to exclude reviewed equipment that degrades to dust, expires or is temporary before comparisons. The next permanent candidate can then win ordinary, prayer, tank, weight and weapon-loadout comparisons, including tied alternatives, tradeoffs, upgrade paths and generated Obtain tasks. Repairable or rechargeable equipment and ammunition remain included. The rule uses existing personal task-rule commands, inherited values, history and Undo; it does not erase saved Obtain records or remove items from the potential identity catalogue or engine source results. The reviewed permanence overlay documents its sources and leaves unknown equipment included.

`buildEquipmentCatalogue(definitions)` is reexported from the presentation module. It registers all potential generated BiS identities before saved tasks are migrated. Labels use the upstream-compatible `Obtain a/an ~|formatted name|~` format, including plurals and `formatted_name` values. IDs use the existing NFC-normalized task identifier. Active rows, equally scored peer catalogue rows, source evidence, explanations, completion scope and declared equip requirements are returned separately from the potential catalogue.

After a member's gameplay migration is recorded, authored rules and task status are authoritative. Clearing a profile override, clearing a backlog flag, or unchecking challenge completion does not reload the preserved legacy value. Legacy rules/backlogs remain fallback input only before conversion; an explicit authored backlog value takes precedence even then.

Weapon damage and accuracy use explicit `damage_tier` / `accuracy_tier` values, falling back to item tier only when positive ability damage / accuracy exists. The picker comparison uses 0.85 times accuracy tier plus damage tier. Two-handed weapons contribute full damage; one-handed loadouts contribute two thirds main-hand and one third off-hand damage. Exact score ties go to the two-handed weapon (one item instead of two). Off-hand accuracy does not determine dual-wield accuracy. Declared special attacks and passives contribute reference bonuses of 2 and 5; augmentable items contribute 10 when the engine grants Invention Guild access. Off-hand bonuses are halved. Imported definitions are never mutated.

Ammunition selectors are expanded through `codeItems.ammoTools` and `itemsPlus`. Required ammunition must itself be engine eligible. Weapon damage is capped by compatible ammunition damage tier. Both ranged hands must support the same equipped ammo item. `No ammo` weapons use their own damage. The consumable-primary rule excludes secondary consumable sources. A weapon without reachable compatible ammunition produces a diagnostic and no usable loadout.

Each style returns its best loadout plus reviewable alternatives for two-handed, dual-wield, one-handed and shield use. Shield defence and life points remain explicit tradeoffs. The 1H/2H and shield display rules retain the corresponding best alternatives as goals. Split stab/slash/crush rules constrain melee weapon styles. Prayer weapon loadouts maximize the combined bonus of both hands instead of damage. Engine-eligible Necromancy trial weapons remain visible in F2P; this deliberately corrects the old blanket suppression of that style. Backlogged gear is removed before comparing so the next available candidate can win. Challenge completion uses the configured group scope without claiming personal completion for another member.

Scores are picker rankings, not target-specific damage per second. Charged equipment displays maintenance without subtracting upkeep from the score. Set effects, relic-energy allocation and individual magic spell loadouts remain limitations. Specialist views compare tank armour/life points, declared skilling priorities, relic and unlock prerequisites, and ordinary/prayer/coupled-loadout upgrade paths. Weight comparisons use reviewed worn weights from the Wiki overlay; 3,019 of 3,550 equipment rows have weights, with unresolved coverage recorded explicitly. These comparisons do not infer inventory or simulate set bonuses. The tests use synthetic declarative fixtures to verify coupled scoring, ammunition dependencies, eligibility boundaries, stable identities and tradeoff rules; they are not observed gameplay validation.

On the captured QXA/QXB fixtures, the authored weapon choices match the original worker's Melee, Ranged, Magic and Prayer choices: corrupt dragon spear; yew shortbow with rune arrow; mystic wand with mystic orb; corrupt dragon mace with Avernic book. Engine-confirmed F2P trial access additionally produces the tier-10 death guard and skull lantern. This read-only snapshot check covered 539 eligible records in approximately 171 ms. Ordinary equipment differences and broader task-engine parity are tracked separately in the reference comparison documentation.

The worker exposes `result.currentBiS` for the Current BiS panel. `obtainable.loadouts` and `obtainable.recommendations` retain the same ranking. Each recommendation also carries `sourceAccess`, computed against a second engine evaluation with shared geography disabled, and `challenge`, computed against the configured completion scope. A source in another member's unlocked chunk is usable under shared access without implying that member owns the item. Once an obtain challenge is satisfied under `any`, the other member does not need to repeat it for challenge credit. Personal quest prerequisites remain personal, and the reference equipment records do not declare tradeability; shared access is not proof that a reward can be transferred.

`currentBiS.owned.known` remains false and `owned.items` remains empty because current inventory and equipped gear are unknown. `owned.recordedObtained` combines explicit personal obtain-task completions with `member.obtainedEquipment`, a saved list of known equipment names recorded by hand. Duplicate evidence produces one row; removing the hand record leaves any personal task evidence. Hand records show **Added by hand** and an own-player **Remove** control, including when membership prevents ranking the item. Challenge-only checkmarks, including migrated picker checks, are deliberately excluded. Both evidence kinds supply personal item requirements, recorded-best rankings and upgrade starting points, subject to membership and equipment requirements; they do not complete a challenge or give the partner the item.

Recording and removal use the revision-checked `set-obtained-equipment` command, History and Undo. The server validates names against its equipment catalogue. Player invitations can edit and undo their own records; administrator links can target either player. Viewer links have no recording controls. The list participates in gameplay fingerprints and full backups; older saves load an empty list. Original-picker group and personal imports preserve existing hand records. `importedSelections` separately preserves legacy `manualEquipment`: the upstream `gatherChunksInfo` function inserts these as additional item sources, and our import already reads them as obtainable-source overrides. They are not converted into personal obtain facts. The upstream Current BiS default itself renders `highestOverall`; its obtained-only toggle is a separate view.

**Hide gear I already have** filters Obtainable using the combined personal records. It leaves missing weapon components and unrecorded tied alternatives visible, and stores its choice in browser preferences without changing gameplay. **Compare with the partner** shows each player's best recorded and obtainable gear by style and slot, with explicit better/equal/unknown wording. Weapons retain their coupled ranking; the view does not infer current possession or tradeability. Partner details reuse the current full-result caches and the duo detail loader, including its shared in-flight request. Each slot stacks player columns on a narrow phone.

The worker also exposes `result.activityInfo`. It indexes the entire evaluated catalogue instead of the next-goal subset: all available skill tasks, primary methods, display alternatives and blocked tasks; grouped quest steps and achievement tasks; other activities; Slayer masters and declared assignment targets; and clue steps/locations by tier. Every row retains source evidence and blocker reasons. Slayer planning uses reachable training ceilings and requires explicit personal quest completion for assignment quest gates; unknown assignment metadata fails closed. Reachable skill levels and possible quest points are planning results, not current character stats. Clue counts do not claim a full-scroll completion probability.

The [cache equipment correction report](research/cache-equipment-corrections.md) documents ID/variant verification, comparable-field corrections, separate worn/inventory weights and the read-only equipment preview. All corrections preserve picker task identities and personal facts.

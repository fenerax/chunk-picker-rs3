# Area check

Use **Area check** in the map toolbar, then drag a rectangle over surface chunks.
Locked chunks are included. On a phone, tap the two corners; dragging still pans.
The selection includes the chunks containing both corners and stops at **128
chunks**. Escape cancels. Keyboard users can pan with the arrow keys and press
Enter twice to choose corners at the pointer or map centre.

The dialog compares the selected area alone with that area added to the current
player's unlocked chunks. Group view offers a player switcher. Combined keeps the
ordinary rules for connected partner territory and supplies; alone clears other
ownership. Both retain personal progress and training rules. **As members** is a
temporary content override. The panel clearly labels membership assumptions.
Nothing is saved, including on view-only links. Long Discord summaries are split
into separately copyable messages below Discord's 2,000-character limit.

## Reading the results

The ranges are the lowest and highest **method entry levels**, with method names.
Entry levels say when a method becomes available; they do not limit how long it
can give XP. Expand Methods to see prerequisites and stretches with no new usable
method.

**Gaps** shows the largest stretch of at least 15 levels for each scope. It checks
the stretches between usable method entry levels.
It also checks the final stretch from the last usable method to the highest skill
requirement among active tasks in that scope: the selected area for **Alone**, or current
chunks plus the selected area for **With current**. Equipment and wield goals
count when the ordinary active task list would show them, including their
`Requirements` fields and category levels; active quest skill requirements count
too. The same selector applies equipment permanence, category rules, membership,
availability, processing, goal ranking and saved alternatives. Backlogged,
completed and shared-satisfied duties are excluded; a satisfied requirement needs
no further challenge grind. Search, Show all and Show blocked are inspection
views and do not change targets. Primary method entry levels alone are not task targets.
If no task needs that skill, there is no gap. If methods already cover the
requirement without a long stretch, the row says no task needs more than that
level. The skill cap may label the bar's end when there is no target, but never
creates a gap.
Each gap names the method you would keep using and the next method, local
requirement that ends the stretch. For example, a 1 → 40 gap means keeping
the level-1 method until the level-40 method opens. It does not mean XP stops at
level 1, and it makes no claim about which method has the best XP rate.

Rows with a gap of 20 levels or more receive shading; the three largest alone
gaps also appear above the table. Blocked and conditional methods remain visible
in details with their reasons, but cannot close a gap. Methods without reachable
sources or supplies are excluded. No usable method recorded means unknown
coverage, not proof that a skill is impossible to train.

The separate **Can't start** line shows where the app has no route from the
player's recorded level to a needed first method or task requirement. It uses the
same scope's highest task requirement and never warns when no task needs the
skill. Without
recorded levels, a members preview can use the reviewed F2P trial cap, explicitly
labelled **F2P reference level**. This reference does not assert personal progress.

The calculation uses the ordinary task engine, skill-guide tasks and the Helper's
training selectors in the existing bounded advisory worker. The reviewed Colossi
placement can also appear as a conditional local method before its quest route
opens; its access and personal quest blockers remain visible. The map remains
interactive during computation. Results are cached by player, revision and
normalized selection; changing player, content assumption or save revision
replaces the result. Closing the dialog aborts its request. Method details render
only when expanded.
